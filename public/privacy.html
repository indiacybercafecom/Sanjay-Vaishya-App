Privacy Policy

This policy describes how SANJAY VAISHYA collects, processes, protects, and deletes information across our software development and client project management platform.

01. Account & Authentication Data

When you register or sign in via Firebase Authentication, we collect your full name, email address, and optional company or phone profile stored in Firebase Realtime Database and our private operational database.

02. Project Inquiries & Uploaded Files

When you submit a project inquiry or upload project briefs, wireframes, or deliverables inside a project room, files are stored in authenticated private storage and accessed only by authorized project participants.

03. Billing & Milestone Transactions

Payments are processed securely via Razorpay. SANJAY VAISHYA verifies transaction signatures server-side and retains payment codes and tax invoices without storing raw card or UPI credentials.

04. How Information Is Used

  • Preparing software quotations, milestones, and technical proposals
  • Providing authenticated client workspaces and real-time project communication
  • We never sell or rent client data to advertisers or third parties

05. Cookies & Session Security

Signed HttpOnly cookies maintain authenticated backend API and WebSocket sessions securely without exposing tokens in URLs. Optional consent preferences are stored locally.

06. Data Deletion & Contact

Request category removal or permanent self-service account deletion anytime on our Data Deletion Portal or write to sanjayvaishya.dev@gmail.com (+91 9203251821).
